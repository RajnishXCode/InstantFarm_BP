What's inside

🌾 Instant Crop Growth

Planted crops are pushed to their fully grown state the moment you place them.

  • Wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroot
  • Melon and pumpkin stems
  • Torchflower and pitcher crops

🌳 Instant Trees

Place a sapling and the matching full-size tree generates almost immediately.

  • Oak, spruce, birch, jungle
  • Acacia, dark oak, cherry
  • Failed growth restores the sapling

🧭 Ore Detector Radar

Hold the Ore Detector in your main hand to scan the area around you every second.

  • 32-block scan radius
  • Real block data, no simulation
  • Distance, direction and height for each vein

Using it

  1. Craft or get the item with /give @s instantfarm:ore_detector.
  2. Hold it in your main hand. The radar appears above the hotbar automatically. No button press is needed.
  3. Switch to another item or empty your hand and the radar disappears.
  4. Turn or walk. The radar updates its directions and distances as you move.

Install

  1. Download the Complete Vanilla+ Add-on file above.
  2. Open it. Minecraft Bedrock imports both packs.
  3. In your world settings, enable both packs under Behavior Packs and Resource Packs.
  4. Restart the game once so the scripts, recipes and textures load.

Notes and limitations

  • The radar is drawn in the actionbar, which is the only HUD text channel the Script API can update. It is text-only and shares that space with other actionbar messages.
  • Ores in chunks that are not loaded are skipped until those chunks load.
  • Updating: install a new version over the old one. The packs use stable UUIDs, so the update replaces the old pack. Restart the game afterwards.
